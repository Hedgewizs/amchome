Smart Home

A multi-room audio distribution system. Audio components built on the same hi-end modules engineered by the AMC Hi-Fi team — so every room gets true high-end sound, whether the whole house plays one source or every room plays its own.

Switchers

Multi-room audio switcher — distribute sources across zones.

AMC XOi

Multi-room audio switcher for flexible zone control.

AMC XGi

Smart Home audio component for multi-room distribution.

AMC X1616e

Flagship multi-room switcher — the heart of larger installations.

Control

AMC Remote App

Control Smart Home switchers (X86i, XOi, X1616e) from mobile devices. HC-03 module required.

AMC XK1

Multi-zone wall-mount keypads — elegant in-room control.

AMC HC-03

Smart Home controller module — enables mobile control of switchers via the Remote App.

AMC XS1bt

Remote Bluetooth module — installs at remote locations via Cat.5, stream music over Bluetooth.
